MR-NavQ95
=========

The MR-NavQ95 is the i.MX 95 vehicle computer of the NXP Mobile Robotics
family (Cortex-A55 compute and Cortex-M7 real-time). Bring up the board, then add
connectivity, on-device AI, and robotics middleware.
